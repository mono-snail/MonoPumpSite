The core idea

A short guided session. A reading with context.

Cover the rear camera and flash with your fingertip. MonoPump observes subtle color changes caused by blood flow and estimates your pulse when the signal is stable.

  1. 01
    Cover and hold still

    The in-app guide helps with lighting, camera coverage, and movement.

  2. 02
    Review the estimate

    See the pulse estimate, signal confidence, and session duration.

  3. 03
    Add context and save

    Label the moment, then keep the reading and quality metadata in local history.

Private by design

Your measurements stay with you.

MonoPump does not require an account or operate cloud storage for your pulse history. Your device remains the source of truth.

Local history Measurements and profile data are stored on your device.
No camera files Camera frames are processed for the estimate, not saved as photos or video.
No account Start using the app without registration or a cloud identity.
Explicit export Your records leave the app only when you choose to export and share them.
